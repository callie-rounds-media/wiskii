03 / Concepts

Three ideas

Each idea is shot alongside a professional photographer and edited in a warm, cinematic style that fits WISKII's world.

Concept 01

The helicopter skydive

I jump from a helicopter in a matching WISKII set and fall through open sky, filmed in freefall for a cinematic edit, while a professional photographer captures editorial stills before and after the jump.

Cinematic Reel and editorial stills

Concept 02 / The wildcard

The helicopter hang

I hang from beneath a helicopter as it flies over the water, dressed in WISKII, while a professional photographer shoots the whole scene from the ground so it reads like a fashion editorial.

Editorial photo carousel

Concept 03

The sunrise balloon jump

I jump from a hot air balloon at sunrise, styled in WISKII's softer colors like Ivory and Capri Serenity, so the early light and the slow climb before the exit become part of the film.

Sunrise Reel

Each concept is scoped and quoted per shoot, because locations, aircraft and permits vary from place to place.
